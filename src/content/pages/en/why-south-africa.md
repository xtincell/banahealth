## Advanced medical technology and leading expertise

South Africa's healthcare system comprises a public and a private sector. While the public sector resembles those of developing countries, the private sector — which receives international patients — competes with the most developed nations.

South Africa's private healthcare system is among the best in the world. A Monitor Group study placed South Africa among the world’s ten strongest systems, behind Australia and Switzerland and ahead of France, the United Kingdom and Germany — a ranking weighing infrastructure and clinical skill alike. Among the country's best-known achievements is the world's first successful human-to-human heart transplant, performed by Dr Christiaan Barnard in 1967.

## Affordable care

A procedure carried out in South Africa commonly costs **30 to 70 % less** than in Western Europe or the United States, for comparable clinical facilities.

The gap reads better as a proportion than as an amount: in vitro fertilisation comes to **roughly a third** of the American price, cosmetic surgery to **a quarter or a third** of its British equivalent. These ratios hold steady over time; the prices themselves do not.

We publish no price list, and that is deliberate: two cases that look alike on paper rarely produce the same quote. Length of stay, preliminary tests and the choice of practitioner all change the total. **Send us your medical report and we will come back with a costed estimate, negotiated with our partner institutions.**

## Short waiting times

Patients in Western Europe and the United States often wait a long time for the care they need. In England, the public waiting list exceeds seven million cases, and nearly four patients in ten are not treated within the official eighteen-week standard. The median wait before treatment begins has climbed from seven to twelve weeks since 2019.

This is not the case in the South African private system, where you will receive a prompt response. A capable medical facilitation provider can secure appointments with respected specialists in a very short time.

## Straightforward visa arrangements

The number of international patients travelling to South Africa has risen considerably, and immigration legislation has recognised the need to accommodate them.

Nationals of several countries are entirely exempt from visa requirements. For those who do need an entry visa, only a few simple requirements must be met to obtain one for medical treatment. Your facilitation provider should supply the necessary documents.

## A destination for care — and for travel

Once your treatment or procedure is behind you, South Africa has a great deal to offer. A treatment at a restful spa, the largest shopping centres in Africa, a safari in a game reserve, or the chance to understand Nelson Mandela's country by visiting Soweto or the Apartheid Museum.

There is much to enjoy while you recover.
