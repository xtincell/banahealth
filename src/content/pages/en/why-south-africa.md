## Advanced medical technology and leading expertise

South Africa's healthcare system comprises a public and a private sector. While the public sector resembles those of developing countries, the private sector — which receives international patients — competes with the most developed nations.

South Africa's private healthcare system is among the best in the world. According to the Pool Stream Database (Monitor Group, 2008), South Africa ranks among the top ten systems, behind Australia and Switzerland and ahead of France, the United Kingdom and Germany. That ranking accounts for both infrastructure and clinical skill. Among the country's best-known achievements is the world's first successful human-to-human heart transplant, performed by Dr Christiaan Barnard in 1967.

## Affordable care

According to the International Federation of Health Plans, South Africa offers highly affordable medical care compared with destinations such as Western Europe or the United States.

A breast augmentation costing 8,000 dollars in the United Kingdom comes to roughly 3,600 dollars in South Africa. Patients in the United States paying around 12,400 dollars for in vitro fertilisation can expect to pay a third of that amount locally.

## Short waiting times

Patients in Western Europe and the United States often endure long waits before receiving the care they need. In the United Kingdom, a patient wishing to see a specialist must first consult a general practitioner; that referral can take up to eighteen weeks.

This is not the case in the South African private system, where you will receive a prompt response. A capable medical facilitation provider can secure appointments with respected specialists in a very short time.

## Straightforward visa arrangements

The number of international patients travelling to South Africa has risen considerably in recent years, and immigration legislation has recognised the need to accommodate them.

Nationals of several countries are entirely exempt from visa requirements. For those who do need an entry visa, only a few simple requirements must be met to obtain one for medical treatment. Your facilitation provider should supply the necessary documents.

## A destination for care — and for travel

Once your treatment or procedure is behind you, South Africa has a great deal to offer. A treatment at a restful spa, the largest shopping centres in Africa, a safari in a game reserve, or the chance to understand Nelson Mandela's country by visiting Soweto or the Apartheid Museum.

There is much to enjoy while you recover.
