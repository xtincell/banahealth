## What we will ask you for

To put together a serious proposal, three things are usually enough: **your most recent medical report**, **what you are looking for** — a second opinion, a procedure, a check-up — and **your scheduling constraints**.

If you have no documents to hand, write to us anyway. We will tell you what to ask your treating doctor for.

## We answer everyone

Including when the answer is that we are not the right people. Some situations are better handled locally; others do not warrant travelling at all. We will say so plainly rather than draw you into a journey that serves no purpose.
