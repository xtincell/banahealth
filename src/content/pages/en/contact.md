## By email

For a first enquiry, write to us at **[hello@banahealth.care](mailto:hello@banahealth.care)**.

If you already have a medical report, attach it to your message: this allows us to direct your case to the right specialists from the very first exchange.

## Opening hours

Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. (South African time, UTC+2).

## Where we are

Our offices and accommodation are in Johannesburg, South Africa, close to the private hospitals we work with.

## What happens next

1. You describe your situation and send us your medical report if you have one.
2. We consult our network and identify the most suitable specialists.
3. We send you a cost estimate and a proposed care pathway.
4. Once you agree, we arrange appointments, visa, flights and accommodation.
