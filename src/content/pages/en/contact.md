## What we will ask of you

To draw up a meaningful proposal, three things are generally sufficient: your most recent **medical report**, the **nature of what you are seeking** — a second opinion, a procedure, a general check-up — and the **time frame your trip allows**.

Should you hold no documentation at present, do write to us nonetheless. We will tell you what to request from your treating doctor.

## We reply to everyone

This includes those instances where our answer is that we are not the right company for you. Some conditions are better attended to locally; others do not warrant travelling at all. We will say so plainly rather than engage you in a journey that serves no purpose.

Our commitment is to make the way ahead clear, whether or not it leads to South Africa.
