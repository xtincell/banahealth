This privacy policy sets out how BanaHealth uses and protects any information that you give us when you use our website. We are committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected. Should we ask you to provide certain information by which you can be identified, you can be assured that it will only be used in accordance with this statement.

## Information This Website Collects

This is a static website. It sets **no cookies**, uses **no audience measurement tools** and carries **no advertising trackers**. No account is created here and no information is entered into it.

Our hosting provider keeps technical connection logs — internet protocol address, date and page requested — which are necessary to operate and secure the service. These logs are not exploited for commercial purposes.

## Information You Give Us

When you write to us or telephone us, we receive the information you choose to share: your name, your contact details, and whatever you tell us of your medical situation.

That information serves one purpose only, which is to assess your request and organize your care. It is never sold, rented or passed on to third parties for commercial purposes.

## Health Information

The medical information you entrust to us is sensitive and is treated as such. It is shared only with the medical practitioners and facilities directly involved in your care, and only to the extent that your care requires.

## Retention

We keep your information for as long as assisting you requires, and thereafter for the period imposed upon us by our legal and accounting obligations. Beyond that, it is deleted.

## Your Rights

You may at any time request access to your information, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to its being processed. Write to us at [hello@banahealth.care](mailto:hello@banahealth.care) and we will reply within one month.

## Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any substantial change will be noted on this page.
