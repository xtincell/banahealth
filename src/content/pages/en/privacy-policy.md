## What this website collects

This is a static website. It sets **no cookies**, uses **no analytics tools** and carries **no advertising trackers**. No accounts are created here and no data is entered.

Our hosting provider keeps technical connection logs — IP address, date, page requested — needed to operate and secure the service. These logs are not used for commercial purposes.

## What we collect when you contact us

When you write to us or call, we receive the information you choose to give: your name, contact details, and whatever you share about your medical situation.

That information is used for one purpose only: to consider your request and arrange your support. It is never sold, rented or passed to third parties for commercial purposes.

## Health data

The medical information you entrust to us is sensitive and is treated as such. It is shared only with the healthcare professionals and institutions directly involved in your care, and only to the extent required for it.

## Retention

We keep your data for as long as your support requires, and thereafter for the period imposed by our legal and accounting obligations. Beyond that, it is deleted.

## Your rights

You may at any time request access to your data, its correction or its deletion, or object to its processing. Write to **[hello@banahealth.care](mailto:hello@banahealth.care)**: we respond within one month.

## Changes

This policy may be updated. Any substantial change will be noted on this page.
