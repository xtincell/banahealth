## Formalities and health

### South African diplomatic representation

The official directory of South African embassies, consulates and high commissions worldwide. This is where you will find the right office for your medical visa application.

[dirco.gov.za](https://dirco.gov.za/south-african-representation-abroad/)

### Health Professions Council of South Africa

The HPCSA is the statutory body regulating health professions in South Africa. Its register lets you confirm that a practitioner is licensed to practise.

[hpcsa.co.za](https://www.hpcsa.co.za/)

### South African Medical Association

SAMA is the professional association for South African doctors, in both the public and private sectors.

[samedical.org](https://www.samedical.org/)

## While you are here

### South African Tourism

The official tourism site, for discovering the country during your recovery.

[southafrica.net](https://www.southafrica.net/)

### Things to do in South Africa

A selection of activities and places to visit, arranged by interest.

[southafrica.net — things to do](https://www.southafrica.net/za/en/travel/category/things-to-do)

### Gauteng Tourism

Practical information on Gauteng province, home to Johannesburg and most of our partner institutions.

[gauteng.net](https://www.gauteng.net/)

### South African Weather Service

The official forecast, useful when packing: Johannesburg sits at 1,750 metres and its evenings are cool even in summer.

[weathersa.co.za](https://www.weathersa.co.za/)
